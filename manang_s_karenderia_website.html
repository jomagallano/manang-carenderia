Ang Aming Kwento

"Kain na, anak! Bawal ang gutom dito sa Manang's."

What started in 2012 as two stainless kalderos on a wooden table outside our gate has grown into our neighborhood's favorite dining spot. Every morning at 4:00 AM, we visit the local palengke to pick the freshest pork, bangus, and gulay.

Whether you're a student stretching your allowance, a commuter needing a quick almusal, or a family picking up hapunan, you'll always get generous servings, free sabaw, and a warm smile.
