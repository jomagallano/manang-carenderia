Ang Kwento ni Manang

Started with two kalderos and a promise to feed the neighborhood well.

Back in 2012, Manang Tessie set up a humble wooden table outside our gate with just two pots—Chicken Adobo and Ginisang Monggo—for tricycle drivers, students, and office workers rushing through their morning commute.

Today, Manang's Karenderia still wakes up at 3:30 AM to visit the wet market for fresh produce, slow-simmers every broth from scratch, and makes sure nobody leaves our counter hungry.
